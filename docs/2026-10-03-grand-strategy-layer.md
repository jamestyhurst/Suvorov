# 2026-10-03 — general grand-strategy layer

Polities, locations, and persons were already the core. This slice adds what those games share besides dynastic rules.

Offices are core. A polity seats a living member in a named office, including a ruler. Diplomacy, forces, intelligence, and fog are opt-in, and both the Crusader Kings-like and Hearts of Iron-like profiles turn them on. Force kind is a game word, so army and fleet are not a closed engine list. An operation starts hidden. Fog off means the map is visible. Fog on means a polity sees its own land and what it has revealed.
